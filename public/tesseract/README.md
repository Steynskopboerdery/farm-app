Text reader for photos of farm maps (tesseract.js 5.1.1, Apache-2.0), served by the app itself so it works
without outside downloads: worker.min.js from tesseract.js/dist, the LSTM cores from tesseract.js-core,
and the English and Afrikaans "best_int" language data from @tesseract.js-data (4.0.0_best_int).
